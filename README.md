# 🚀 PathPilot AI
### Don't just choose your future. Try it first.

PathPilot AI is an AI-powered career exploration platform that helps students discover and experience different career paths through interactive challenges, personalized guidance, and AI-driven feedback.

🔗 **Live Demo:** https://pathh-1.onrender.com  
📂 **GitHub Repository:** https://github.com/vmanaswini-14/pathh

## ✨ Features

- 💻 **Software Engineer:** Practice coding challenges and evaluate your problem-solving skills.
- 📊 **Data Analyst:** Explore SQL challenges and develop data analysis skills.
- 🎨 **UX/Product Designer:** Experience design-oriented tasks and explore product thinking.
- 🤖 **AI-Powered Feedback:** Receive feedback and guidance to improve your performance.
- ⚖️ **Career Comparison:** Compare career paths and explore opportunities.
- 🗺️ **Personalized Roadmaps:** Get guidance to help you move toward your career goals.
- 🔐 **Authentication:** Firebase-powered user authentication.
